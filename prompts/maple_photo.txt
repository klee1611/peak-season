If it does, judge how far autumn colour has progressed, using exactly one of these stage ids:
$stages

Guidance:
- green: leaves are essentially all green. Light green, yellow-green, young leaves, sunlit or
  backlit green leaves, and variegated leaves with white or cream edges all count as green.
- turning: some leaves are clearly yellow, orange or red, but many are still green.
- peak: most leaves are vivid yellow, orange or red and still on the tree.
- past_peak: leaves are brown or faded, or most have fallen and branches are bare.

Bright sunlight and shadows are not autumn colour. If you are unsure between green and turning,
choose green.

progress is how far along the season is, from 0.0 (fully green) to 1.0 (bare branches).
confidence is how sure you are, from 0.0 to 1.0.
